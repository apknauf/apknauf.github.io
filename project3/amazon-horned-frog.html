Amazon Horned Frog

Ceratophrys cornuta

A large, broad-bodied frog that uses camouflage and ambush tactics.

Back to Amphibians

Habitat

Humid lowland rainforest and moist leaf litter.

Diet

Insects and other small animals; larger individuals can take substantial prey.

Behavior

Often remains buried in leaf litter and waits for prey to approach.

Geographic Range

Found in parts of the western and central Amazon region.

Conservation

Forest and leaf-litter habitat protection is important.

Quick Facts

  • Its horn-like projections help break up the outline of the head.
  • It has a very wide mouth for an amphibian.
  • Camouflage is a major part of its hunting strategy.
