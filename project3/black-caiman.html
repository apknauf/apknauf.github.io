Reptiles

Black Caiman

Melanosuchus niger

A large crocodilian that occupies rivers, floodplains, and other freshwater habitats.

Back to Reptiles

Habitat

Slow rivers, lagoons, flooded forests, and wetlands.

Diet

Fish, birds, reptiles, and mammals, with prey varying by age.

Behavior

Often waits motionless near the water's edge and becomes more active after dark.

Geographic Range

Native to much of the Amazon Basin.

Conservation

Wetland protection and sustainable management of wildlife are important to recovery and long-term survival.

Quick Facts

  • Black caimans are among the largest caimans.
  • Young animals eat smaller prey than adults.
  • They play an important role as freshwater predators.
