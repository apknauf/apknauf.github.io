Mammals

Capybara

Hydrochoerus hydrochaeris

The world's largest living rodent, closely tied to freshwater habitats.

Back to Mammals

Habitat

Riverbanks, wetlands, flooded forests, and grassy areas near water.

Diet

Grasses, aquatic plants, and other vegetation.

Behavior

Social and semi-aquatic. Groups often rest near water and use swimming to escape danger.

Geographic Range

Widely distributed across northern and central South America.

Conservation

They are relatively adaptable, but healthy wetlands and freshwater systems remain important.

Quick Facts

  • Capybaras can remain underwater for several minutes.
  • They commonly live in groups.
  • Their feet are partly webbed.
