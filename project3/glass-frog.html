Amphibians

Glass Frog

Centrolenidae (family)

Small tree frogs known for translucent skin and a life closely connected to streams.

Back to Amphibians

Habitat

Humid forests near clear streams and small rivers.

Diet

Small insects and other tiny invertebrates.

Behavior

Mostly nocturnal and often found on leaves above flowing water.

Geographic Range

Glass frogs are diverse across tropical Central and South America, including the Amazon region.

Conservation

Clean streams and intact forest are important for their reproductive cycle.

Quick Facts

  • Some species have translucent skin through which organs can be seen.
  • Eggs are commonly laid on vegetation above streams.
  • Tadpoles drop into the water after hatching.
