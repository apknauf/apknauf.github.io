Reptiles

Green Anaconda

Eunectes murinus

A huge semi-aquatic constrictor that spends much of its time in and around water.

Back to Reptiles

Habitat

Swamps, marshes, flooded forests, slow rivers, and oxbow lakes.

Diet

Fish, birds, turtles, and a variety of mammals.

Behavior

Usually ambushes prey from water and constricts it before swallowing.

Geographic Range

Found across much of tropical northern South America.

Conservation

Wetland and river protection helps maintain their habitat and prey base.

Quick Facts

  • Green anacondas are excellent swimmers.
  • Females are much larger than males.
  • Their eyes and nostrils are positioned high on the head.
