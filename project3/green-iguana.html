Reptiles

Green Iguana

Iguana iguana

A tree-dwelling lizard commonly associated with warm tropical forests and rivers.

Back to Reptiles

Habitat

Forest edges, riverbanks, and trees close to water.

Diet

Mostly leaves, shoots, flowers, and fruit.

Behavior

Basks in warm areas and can dive or swim to escape predators.

Geographic Range

Occurs widely through tropical Central and South America.

Conservation

Forest and riparian habitat protection helps populations remain stable.

Quick Facts

  • Green iguanas are strong swimmers.
  • They use a long tail for balance and defense.
  • Juveniles often spend more time on lower vegetation.
