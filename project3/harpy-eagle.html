Harpy Eagle

Harpia harpyja

A large forest raptor with powerful talons adapted for hunting in the canopy.

Back to Birds

Habitat

Mature tropical forests with tall trees suitable for nesting.

Diet

Mostly arboreal mammals and large birds.

Behavior

Uses powerful flight and surprise attacks to hunt among trees.

Geographic Range

The species occurs through parts of Central and South America, including the Amazon.

Conservation

Large areas of mature forest are important for nesting and hunting.

Quick Facts

  • Harpy eagles have extremely strong feet and talons.
  • They build large nests high in trees.
  • They are among the largest living eagles.
