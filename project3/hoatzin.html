Birds

Hoatzin

Opisthocomus hoazin

A leaf-eating bird strongly associated with riverside and seasonally flooded vegetation.

Back to Birds

Habitat

Wet forests, swamps, and vegetation along slow-moving rivers and lagoons.

Diet

Mostly leaves, with some flowers and fruits.

Behavior

Often rests in groups near water. Young birds can use clawed wing digits to move through vegetation.

Geographic Range

Occurs across parts of the northern and central Amazon Basin and adjacent tropical South America.

Conservation

Protection of wetlands and riverside vegetation helps preserve suitable habitat.

Quick Facts

  • Hoatzins have a specialized digestive system for fermenting leaves.
  • They are poor fliers compared with many other birds.
  • Young birds have temporary claws on their wings.
