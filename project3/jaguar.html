Mammals

Jaguar

Panthera onca

A powerful rainforest cat that helps regulate prey populations.

Back to Mammals

Habitat

Dense forests, wetlands, river edges, and seasonally flooded areas.

Diet

An opportunistic carnivore that can take fish, reptiles, birds, and mammals.

Behavior

Usually solitary. Jaguars are strong swimmers and often travel along rivers.

Geographic Range

The Amazon Basin is one of the most important strongholds for the species.

Conservation

Main conservation concerns include habitat loss, fragmentation, conflict with people, and loss of prey.

Quick Facts

  • Jaguars are the largest cats in the Americas.
  • They have an exceptionally powerful bite.
  • Their rosettes often contain small spots in the center.
