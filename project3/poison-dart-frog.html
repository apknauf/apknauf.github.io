Amphibians

Poison Dart Frog

Dendrobatidae (family)

A group of colorful frogs famous for chemical defenses and rainforest lifestyles.

Back to Amphibians

Habitat

Moist tropical forests, leaf litter, and vegetation near streams.

Diet

Mostly very small insects and other invertebrates.

Behavior

Many species are active by day and use bright colors as warning signals.

Geographic Range

Dart frog species occur across tropical Central and South America; several occur in Amazonian and neighboring forests.

Conservation

Protecting clean, humid forest habitat is essential because amphibians are sensitive to environmental change.

Quick Facts

  • Not every poison dart frog is highly toxic in the wild.
  • Diet can influence the chemicals present in some species.
  • Many species care for their eggs and tadpoles.
