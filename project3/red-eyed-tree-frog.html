Amphibians

Red-eyed Tree Frog

Agalychnis callidryas

A bright green tree frog with vivid eyes and strong climbing ability.

Back to Amphibians

Habitat

Lowland tropical forests and vegetation near ponds or streams.

Diet

Mostly insects and other small invertebrates.

Behavior

Nocturnal and arboreal. It uses sticky toe pads to cling to leaves.

Geographic Range

Native to parts of Central America and northern South America.

Conservation

Forest loss and changes to breeding habitat can affect local populations.

Quick Facts

  • The large eyes may help startle predators.
  • Its toe pads help it climb smooth vegetation.
  • Breeding often occurs around temporary or permanent water.
