Red-footed Tortoise

Chelonoidis carbonarius

A land tortoise recognized by the reddish scales on its legs and head.

Back to Reptiles

Habitat

Forest, savanna, and forest-edge environments with access to cover and food.

Diet

A broad plant-based diet including leaves, fruits, flowers, and fallen vegetation.

Behavior

Mostly terrestrial and active during warm, humid periods.

Geographic Range

Occurs in northern and central South America, including the Amazon region.

Conservation

Habitat conversion and illegal collection can affect populations.

Quick Facts

  • The shell can show yellow, orange, or red markings.
  • Red-footed tortoises use scent and visual cues to communicate.
  • They can live for many decades.
