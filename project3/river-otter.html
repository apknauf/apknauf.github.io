Giant River Otter

Pteronura brasiliensis

A social aquatic mammal adapted to rivers, lakes, and slow-moving waterways.

Back to Mammals

Habitat

Freshwater rivers, oxbow lakes, flooded forests, and wetlands.

Diet

Mostly fish, with crustaceans and other aquatic prey also taken.

Behavior

Highly social. Families cooperate while traveling, hunting, and raising pups.

Geographic Range

Occurs in parts of the Amazon, Orinoco, and La Plata river systems.

Conservation

Pollution, habitat loss, overfishing, and historical hunting have affected populations.

Quick Facts

  • Giant river otters can form family groups.
  • They use vocalizations to communicate.
  • They are strong swimmers with streamlined bodies.
