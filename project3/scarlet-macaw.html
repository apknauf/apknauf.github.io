Birds

Scarlet Macaw

Ara macao

A brightly colored parrot known for strong social bonds and loud calls.

Back to Birds

Habitat

Lowland rainforest, forest edges, and river corridors.

Diet

Fruit, seeds, nuts, and other plant material.

Behavior

Social and highly vocal, often traveling in pairs or small groups.

Geographic Range

Found from southern Mexico through much of northern South America.

Conservation

Habitat loss and capture for the wildlife trade are major concerns in parts of their range.

Quick Facts

  • Scarlet macaws have strong curved bills.
  • They can live for decades.
  • Pairs often remain closely bonded.
