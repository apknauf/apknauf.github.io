Southern Two-toed Sloth

Choloepus didactylus

A slow-moving tree dweller that spends much of its life in the canopy.

Back to Mammals

Habitat

Lowland and flooded tropical forests, especially areas with abundant trees.

Diet

Mostly leaves, flowers, and other plant material.

Behavior

Arboreal and generally solitary. It is more active at night and can swim when needed.

Geographic Range

Found across much of northern South America, including large areas of the Amazon.

Conservation

Forest protection is important because sloths depend on connected tropical habitat.

Quick Facts

  • Two-toed sloths have long curved claws.
  • Their slow metabolism helps conserve energy.
  • Algae can grow in their coarse fur, helping them blend into vegetation.
