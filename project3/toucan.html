Toco Toucan

Ramphastos toco

A large toucan recognized by its oversized colorful bill.

Back to Birds

Habitat

Forest edges, riverine woodland, savanna-forest mosaics, and tropical forests.

Diet

Fruit is important, along with insects, eggs, and small animals.

Behavior

Moves through the canopy and uses its bill to reach food.

Geographic Range

Broadly distributed in South America, including parts of the Amazon Basin.

Conservation

Maintaining forest and tree-cavity habitat supports breeding populations.

Quick Facts

  • The bill is large but relatively light because of its internal structure.
  • Toco toucans are strong fliers despite their bulky appearance.
  • They swallow fruit whole.
