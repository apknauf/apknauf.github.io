How It Works

  1. Sign up or log in with your email and a password.
  2. Pick an animal group in the Wildlife Explorer.
  3. Choose an animal to read about its habitat, diet, and conservation.
  4. Send us a message from the Contact page with questions or ideas.

Why Explore the Amazon?

The Amazon is one of Earth's most important ecosystems. It supports an extraordinary range of wildlife, influences regional water cycles, and supports millions of people.
